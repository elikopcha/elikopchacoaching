Why It Works

Why the foods are mostly animal-based.

Animal foods like meat, eggs, and dairy are the most nutrient-dense things you can eat. Your body absorbs them well, they digest cleanly, and the combination of protein and fat keeps you full in a way that processed food simply doesn't.

Fruit covers the carbohydrate side without the need for grains or seed oils. It gives you quick energy without the blood sugar swings you get from bread or cereal. Eating this way makes it pretty easy to stay in a calorie range without having to track anything, because the foods themselves are naturally filling and low in junk.
