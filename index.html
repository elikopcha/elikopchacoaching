Questions

A few things people usually ask.

Who is this for?
Adults who want to look and feel better but have struggled to find something realistic enough to stick with. If other programs felt too strict, too time-consuming, or just too far from how you actually live — this is built differently. Short workouts, flexible nutrition, and a plan that works around your life, not the other way around.
What do I have to do?
Train 2 to 4 days a week, 30 to 60 minutes per session. The nutrition is straightforward — eat when you're hungry, hit your protein, and don't overcomplicate the rest. Check in with me weekly so I can see what's working and adjust as needed. That's really the whole thing.
What if it doesn't work for me?
The plan is simple enough that sticking to it isn't the hard part. And if you're on the Full Transformation plan, I'm with you until you hit your goal — so you're never left figuring it out alone.
On a GLP-1?

I've used GLP-1s myself, so I understand what they actually do — and what they don't. They're effective at suppressing appetite and dropping weight, but without the right training and nutrition alongside them, a lot of that weight comes from muscle. That's what leads to the skinny-fat look people end up with after losing weight on these medications: lower on the scale, but still soft, still not looking the way they expected.

There's also a real concern about staying on them indefinitely. Most people don't want to be dependent on a medication forever, and they shouldn't have to be. The goal with my GLP-1 clients is to use the medication as a tool to get to their goal weight, while building the muscle and habits that make it possible to maintain their results after they stop — and actually get off the medication for good.

If you're currently on a GLP-1 or considering one, I can build your plan around it.
