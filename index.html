The Problems

The scale is going down, but the mirror hasn't caught up.

Some weight has come off. But you still don't see the body you were expecting — and that's not a discipline problem. Most people on GLP-1s are missing the system that turns weight loss into an actual body transformation.

The Problem

GLP-1s dramatically cut your appetite — which means most people aren't eating enough protein or getting the nutrients their body needs. The result is muscle loss and a body that looks smaller, not better.

How This Fixes It

The nutrition plan here is built around a suppressed appetite. Simple, high-protein foods that keep your muscle intact and your body looking the way you want — without forcing you to eat more than you're comfortable with.

The Problem

Most people on GLP-1s either skip resistance training entirely or do it without a real plan. Without it, a significant chunk of what you're losing is muscle — leaving you lighter but soft.

How This Fixes It

This program includes a 2–4 day training plan designed specifically to build and protect muscle while you lose fat, so what's left actually looks athletic and defined.

You're scared you will regain all of the weight once you come off of the GLP-1.

Coming off GLP-1s without the right habits already in place almost always leads to the weight coming back. The medication suppresses appetite — but it doesn't build the systems that keep you lean for life.

The Problem

Most people rely entirely on the medication to manage their weight. No nutrition habits. No training routine. No sustainable systems. So when they stop, everything comes back — sometimes faster than it came off.

How This Fixes It

This program is designed to build the habits that outlast the medication. By the time you come off your GLP-1, you'll already have the nutrition knowledge, training routine, and mindset to maintain your results permanently.

You're still hungry and getting cravings — even on a GLP-1.

GLP-1s reduce appetite, but they don't eliminate hunger or cravings entirely. Without the right eating strategy, you're still white-knuckling it through the day and feeling guilty every time a craving wins.

The Problem

Most diet approaches tell you to track every calorie, hit exact macros, and stick to rigid meal plans. That's exhausting on a normal appetite — let alone an unpredictable one. The result is constant mental friction, guilt around food, and an approach that's impossible to sustain.

How This Fixes It

This program uses an intuitive eating approach — eat when you're hungry, stop when you're not. No calorie counting, no food guilt, no rigid rules. Just simple guidelines that work with your appetite, keep cravings manageable, and make losing weight feel far less like a battle.
