The Problem vs. The Solution

Your GLP-1 Has Been Letting You Down.

You were promised high energy and a toned body, but you got none of those. You're not alone — thousands of people just like you have experienced the same letdown. Here's what's actually going wrong, and how this coaching system fixes it.

Problem 01
They become weak and frail.

When the scale drops without proper resistance training, a significant portion of that weight loss comes from muscle — not fat. The result is a body that looks and feels worse than before. This program uses a simple 2–4 day per week strength training plan, specifically designed to preserve and build muscle while you lose fat. The workouts are short, repeatable, and built around exercises you can actually stick to long-term — not complicated routines that burn you out in two weeks.

Problem 02
They have low energy due to improper nutrition.

GLP-1 medications suppress your appetite dramatically, which makes it easy to barely eat — and even easier to under-eat protein, the single most important nutrient for energy and muscle retention. The nutrition plan is built specifically around a suppressed appetite, with simple high-protein foods that are easy to prepare and easy to stay consistent with. No calorie counting. No complicated meal plans. Just a clear, sustainable way of eating that keeps your energy up and your muscle intact.

Problem 03
They feel lost in the gym.

Most fitness programs are built for athletes or 25-year-olds chasing aesthetics — not for adults on GLP-1s who want to feel healthy and capable without fitness consuming their life. Walking into a gym without a clear plan is overwhelming, and that uncertainty is exactly what kills consistency. This coaching system removes all the guesswork with a minimalistic, easy-to-follow workout plan tailored to your schedule, your body, and your goals. You always know exactly what to do, which makes showing up — and staying consistent — far easier.
